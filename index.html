Projects

InfoConcept Capsule — Educational YouTube Channel

Created and managed an educational YouTube channel producing short-form concept explainer videos for STEM learners, simplifying complex ideas into accessible content

Video Production, Script Writing, Visual Design, Canva, YouTube Analytics

Platform: YouTube

Channel: @InfoConceptCapsule

Focus: STEM Concept Explainers

A self-initiated educational content project aimed at making STEM concepts accessible to curious learners. Key aspects:

  • Content Strategy: Short-form videos (3–7 minutes) explaining one concept at a time, designed for clarity and retention.
  • Production: End-to-end video production — scripting, recording, editing, and publishing.
  • Pedagogy: Videos follow a hook–explain–apply–reflect structure aligned with principles of multimedia learning.
  • Audience: Primarily secondary and early undergraduate learners looking for concept clarity.
